Filming tips for better listings
  • Face a window or lamp so light falls on the items, not behind you.
  • Use a plain wall or sheet as the background.
  • Hold each item up in the middle of the frame, front then back, and pause for a second.
  • Show tags and any flaws up close.
  • Say the brand, size and any flaws out loud. Narration support is coming next.
  • Put the item down out of view before picking up the next one.
